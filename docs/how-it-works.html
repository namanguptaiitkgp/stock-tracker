A personal markets assistant

It does the stock homework, so you don't have to.

stock-tracker keeps an eye on the Indian stocks you care about and does the same research a diligent investor would do by hand — reading the news, checking the fundamentals, and watching what big investors are quietly doing — then hands you a plain-English summary every morning.

Built on your own Zerodha account. It never trades on its own without you.

Every morning, before the market opens

Think of it as an analyst who comes in at dawn, reads everything overnight, and leaves a briefing on your desk.

  1. It looks at every stock on your watchlist — plus everything you already own in your Zerodha account.
  2. For each one it reads the latest news and decides whether it sounds good, bad, or neutral.
  3. It checks the fundamentals — is the company cheap or expensive, growing or shrinking, healthy or stretched.
  4. It sees what large investors did — promoters, big funds, and known heavyweight traders — and whether they're buying or selling.
  5. It writes you a “Today” brief: the market mood, how your portfolio looks, the news that actually matters, and clear suggested actions — e.g. “nothing urgent” or “review this holding”.

Your watchlist is the heart of it

You add the stocks you care about. For each one, the app keeps four things fresh and in one place.

📈

Price & fundamentals

Live price from your broker, plus valuation, growth and financial-health metrics — so you can tell at a glance if a name is cheap, pricey, or risky.

📰

News, already read for you

Recent headlines are pulled in and each one is auto-labelled positive, negative or neutral — so you see the mood without reading twenty articles.

🧭

Peer companies

It automatically finds 6–8 genuine competitors for any stock, so you can compare like-for-like. It's tuned for Indian quirks — near-monopolies (IRCTC, CDSL), PSUs vs private, conglomerate arms.

🐋

What large investors are doing

The “smart money” tracker (below) watches the big players and tells you whether serious money is quietly accumulating a stock — or heading for the exit.

Tracking the large investors

Big investors have to disclose their trades. The app collects those public disclosures and boils them down to three simple readings per stock.

Conviction Is serious money quietly buying? Promoter purchases, insider buys, and steady institutional accumulation push this up.
Flow Which way is the money moving? Big one-off deals, foreign & domestic institutional activity, and mutual-fund buying/selling.
Red flag Any warning signs? Promoters selling or pledging shares, governance issues — the things that should make you pause.

From these, every stock gets a plain label — Accumulation (worth a look), Distribution (money leaving), or Avoid (red flags). It also tracks well-known heavyweight investors (“sharks”), so you can literally follow a big name's trades over time. When there isn't enough disclosure data on a stock, it says so honestly instead of guessing.

Where the data comes from (“scraping”)

The app gathers information the way a person would — by visiting public websites and reading them — except it does it automatically and files everything neatly into a database. That automatic reading is called scraping.

To be fair to these sources and to stay fast, results are cached for a few hours — the same page is never fetched over and over. Scrapers are also wrapped so that if one website changes its layout, the rest of the morning scan still runs.

The AI does the reading — cheaply

Reading thousands of data points daily would be expensive if you used a big AI model for everything. So it works in two tiers:

⚡

The quick pass (everything)

A small, cheap model (Gemini Flash-Lite) skims every watchlist stock daily and scores how interesting it looks — fast and low-cost.

🔎

The deep dive (the shortlist)

Only the most interesting names get a full write-up from a more capable model. Spending is capped per day, and any paid analysis needs your explicit tap.

The frontend talks only to the FastAPI backend; the backend fans out to the database, cache, broker APIs and AI providers. Long-running work is handed to Celery via Redis.

Data flows

  1. Frontend calls the backend with the user's JWT attached.
  2. FastAPI validates the token, reads through portfolio_cache so concurrent widgets share one Kite fan-out.
  3. Data comes from Kite (market) + cached scrapers (news, FII/DII, fundamentals).
  4. Reads/writes hit TimescaleDB; hot values cached in Redis.
  1. Celery beat fires run_daily_analysis at 08:30 IST.
  2. Worker iterates the universe per-symbol under NullPool.
  3. Gemini Flash-Lite screens each symbol and scores interest.
  4. The shortlist routes to the deep-analysis provider; decisions surface on the Today page.
  1. Ingest SEBI/NSE disclosures: insider trades, bulk/block deals, shareholding patterns, corporate announcements, FII/DII, AMFI MF activity.
  2. Each run is audited + validated; client names normalized once for shark linkage.
  3. rollup.py produces conviction / flow / red-flag scores per (symbol, window, as_of); composite derived.
  4. Coverage diagnostic gates thin data to NO_SIGNAL; Action Summary → Accumulation / Distribution / Avoid.
  1. Bulk screen on Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite.
  2. Deep analysis via a provider-agnostic registry (app/ai/registry.py) — Gemini Vertex default; Anthropic/OpenAI scaffolded.
  3. Per-user daily budget cap; paid calls require explicit UI intent.
  4. All provider credentials stored Fernet-encrypted at rest.
